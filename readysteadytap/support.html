How do two players duel?

Choose a mode, pick 2 Players and lay the device flat between you. The screen splits and each player taps only their own half. On a Mac, choose Keys vs Mouse or Split Keys.

What's a jump start?

Pressing before the signal, or within 100 ms of it, is too fast to be a reaction. The first player to jump start loses the round, but the round runs on so the other player still gets their time.

What's a photo finish?

When both players' times show the same millisecond, the game replays both touches in slow motion and decides the round at 0.1 ms. Only identical times are a dead heat.

I bought Pro but it's locked

Open the Pro screen (the sparkle button in the menu) and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Pro is shared with your Family Sharing group and works on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Why is Haptic Start unavailable?

It reacts to a vibration, so it needs an iPhone's haptic engine. iPad and Mac don't have one.

How do I reset my stats?

Settings → Reset Stats. Game Center leaderboards are not affected.
