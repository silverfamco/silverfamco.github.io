READY STEADY TAP

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Ready Steady Tap is a reaction-time game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It does not collect, store or share any personal data on our servers. We don't have servers.

What stays on your device

Your scores, reaction times, settings and game history are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They never leave it unless you choose to export them as a CSV file. You can erase them at any time in Settings → Reset Stats, or by deleting the app.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your best solo results are submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. That is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see your Apple Account.

Purchases

Ready Steady Tap Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. We receive no payment details or personal information.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: silverfamcorp@gmail.com.
