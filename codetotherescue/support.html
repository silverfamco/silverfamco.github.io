Support

Help for Code To The Rescue on iPhone, iPad and Mac

Contact

Email silverfamcorp@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. Please mention your device and, if something went wrong, the mission you were playing.

How do I play?

Pick a mode, open a mission and open its program. Drag commands such as Forward, Turn and Action into the program, then press ▶. Your rescuers carry out one command per step. If the run goes wrong, step through it one command at a time to see where, fix the block and run again. A short tutorial plays on the first mission, and a tip appears the first time something new shows up.

How do I see the tutorial again?

On the start screen, tap Replay tutorial. Your mission progress is kept.

Which missions are free?

Each of the three modes (Solo Rescue, Rescue Squad and Bug Hunt) has 25 missions, and the first 5 of every mode are free. A mode's pack unlocks its missions 6–25, and the bundle unlocks all three modes. Purchases work with Family Sharing and on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

I bought a pack on another device. How do I get it here?

Open Settings (the gear on the start screen) and choose Restore Purchases under For parents. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

Does my progress sync?

Yes. With iCloud on, progress and stars sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud account. Progress made on two devices is combined, so nothing is lost.

Can I change the language?

Yes. In Settings, choose Language. The game speaks 14 languages and switches the next time it opens.

Why do some buttons ask a maths question?

Purchases and links that leave the game sit behind a parental gate, so they're meant for a grown-up. Answer the question to continue.

Privacy

The game collects no personal data, shows no ads and has no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
