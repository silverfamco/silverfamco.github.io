CODE TO THE RESCUE

Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Code To The Rescue is a coding game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It's designed to collect nothing about you or your child.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that collects data. We don't receive any information about who plays or how they play.

What stays on your device

Your mission progress, stars and settings are saved on the device so you can pick up where you left off.

Services provided by Apple

Some features use Apple services. Apple handles that data under Apple's Privacy Policy, and we can't see it.

  • iCloud: if you're signed in to iCloud, your saved progress syncs through your own iCloud account so it follows you to your other devices.
  • Game Center: if you're signed in to Game Center, achievements and leaderboard scores are reported to Apple under your Game Center profile.
  • In-app purchases: purchases are processed by the App Store. The game only learns which missions you've unlocked.

You can sign out of iCloud or Game Center, or turn off in-app purchases with Screen Time, and the game still works.

Children

The game is made for children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
